# DilDuel store listings

Texts for the App Store and Google Play. Each field shows its character limit
in the heading (`max N`); `scripts/check_store_listing.py` checks them.

The German and Azerbaijani texts were written for this project and should get
a native read-through before they are submitted.

## App Review notes

```
DilDuel is a German–Azerbaijani dictionary. No login is required: all dictionary, training, quiz and listening features work as a guest. Signing in (Google, Apple or email) is optional and only syncs bookmarks and progress across devices.

Account deletion: Settings > profile > delete account. It also works on the web at https://elvinmammadov.github.io/DilDuel/delete-account/.

On first launch the app may show the iOS tracking permission prompt and, in the EU/UK, Google's consent form for ads. The app shows an AdMob banner above the bottom navigation.
```

DAHA ÇOX
